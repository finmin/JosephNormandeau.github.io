03 / Projects

Engineering Work

Aerodynamics Lab
Wind Tunnel Airfoil Analysis

Used surface-pressure measurements and MATLAB integration to calculate lift and drag for NACA 0012 and NACA 4412 airfoils. The results captured zero lift for the symmetric airfoil at 0° and positive lift from the cambered airfoil.

MATLAB Data Analysis NACA Airfoils
View project report (PDF)
Experimental Aerodynamics
Wake Profile Drag Analysis

Calibrated a hot-wire anemometer and integrated measured wake velocity deficits to estimate zero-lift drag. The resulting drag coefficients were 0.0064 for the NACA 0012 and 0.0099 for the NACA 4412.

Hot-Wire Anemometry MATLAB Momentum Analysis
View project report (PDF)
Finite-Wing Aerodynamics
Winglet Performance Study

Compared winglet location, length, and angle across Reynolds numbers of approximately 150,000 and 300,000. MATLAB processing included gravity corrections, uncertainty propagation, lift curves, and drag polars.

Wind Tunnel Testing MATLAB Uncertainty Analysis
View project report (PDF)
Compressible Flow & Propulsion
de Laval Nozzle Flow Analysis

Investigated compressible flow through a converging-diverging nozzle while independently varying inlet and outlet pressure. The team used pressure-tap data to calculate Mach number, velocity, density, mass flow, nozzle force, choking behavior, and shock location.

Compressible Flow Nozzle Testing Shock Analysis
View project report (PDF)
High-Speed Aerodynamics Lab
Shock Tube Experiment

Measured shock-wave propagation with pressure transducers and used the time history to estimate shock speed. I analyzed the data in MATLAB and compared the result with normal-shock and Rankine–Hugoniot relations.

MATLAB Pressure Transducers Shock Dynamics

Aerospace Structures Lab
Structural Mechanics Testing

Tested aluminum and steel specimens in tension through failure, then used strain-gauge measurements to calculate material properties such as Young’s modulus. I compared the experimental values with published reference data and examined likely sources of error.

Tensile Testing Strain Gauges Materials Science

Orbital Mechanics
Lambert Intercept Trajectory

Propagated a target spacecraft with the two-body equations of motion, solved a 45-minute Lambert transfer numerically, and calculated the interceptor’s departure burn. The MATLAB solution produced a required delta-v magnitude of approximately 3.00 km/s and a three-dimensional trajectory visualization.

MATLAB Lambert Solver Two-Body Propagation
